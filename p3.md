# P3: Beta

## Core User Journey

Maya finds a black Casio calculator in the library before its owner has reported
it missing. She creates an account, logs in, and opens **Find an Owner**. She
enters its characteristics, possible loss-time interval, and location. No match
appears, so she selects **Save for later**. In **Find an Owner → Saved found
items**, she reviews her private record, sees zero potential matches, and
uses **Edit** to correct its location.

Later, Alex creates an account and submits a private missing report for his
calculator. He edits its title to make it clearer. When Maya next opens her
saved records or selects **Refresh matches**, she sees one potential
match. **View matches** shows Alex's updated title, category, time interval, and
location, without revealing his private brand or color details.

Maya selects **Contact owner**. The conversation shows the report title and
Alex's username; Alex sees Maya's username in **Messages**. They exchange
identifying details and arrange a handoff. Contacting Alex again about the same
report reopens their existing conversation with its messages preserved.

After the return, Alex opens **Confirm return** below the message composer and
selects **I received my item**. Maya selects **I returned the item**. Once both
confirm, Maya
receives one point, Alex's missing report closes, and it disappears from matching.
Her linked saved found record closes automatically too. Either person can confirm
first, with one confirmation each. Their conversation remains accessible for a final thank-you. Each user's point total is visible with the
explanation **Earn 1 point per jointly confirmed return.**

Owners can still manually close reports they no longer need. Manual closure
awards no points and does not prevent the participants from finishing
return confirmation later.

Closed records are hidden initially. **Show closed** reveals them for read-only
review; **Close report** asks for confirmation before closing either kind.
