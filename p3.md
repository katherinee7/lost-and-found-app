# P3: Beta

## Core User Journey

Maya finds a black Casio calculator in the library before its owner has reported
it missing. She creates an account, logs in, and opens **Find an Owner**. She
enters its characteristics, possible loss-time interval, and location. No match
appears, so she selects **Save for later**. In **My Reports → Saved found
records**, she reviews her private record, sees zero potential matches, and
uses **Edit** to correct its location.

Later, Alex creates an account and submits a private missing report for his
calculator. He edits its title to make it clearer. When Maya next opens her
saved records or selects **Refresh records and matches**, she sees one potential
match. **View matches** shows Alex's updated title, category, time interval, and
location, without revealing his private brand or color details.

Maya selects **Contact owner**. The conversation shows the report title and
Alex's username; Alex sees Maya's username in **Messages**. They exchange
identifying details and arrange a handoff. Contacting Alex again about the same
report reopens their existing conversation with its messages preserved.

After the return, Alex closes his missing report. It remains read-only in his
list and disappears from searches and Maya's matches on refresh. Maya separately
closes her found record. Their conversation remains accessible for a final
thank-you.
