# P2: MVP

## Core User Journey

Starting with an empty database, Alex creates a Backtrack account, logs in, and submits a private report for a missing black Casio calculator, including a possible loss-time interval and locations. Maya, who found a calculator, creates her own account and searches using its characteristics. Backtrack identifies Alex’s compatible report and displays a limited summary without revealing its brand or colors. Maya selects “Contact owner” to start a private conversation. Alex opens his Messages page, and the two exchange descriptions to assess ownership and arrange a handoff. After recovering the calculator, Alex closes his report, which remains in his own report list but no longer appears in finder searches.
